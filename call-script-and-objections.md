# Cold Call Script and Objection Handling

## Opener
> "Hi [First name], this is Rudolf with Harvest Yield. I know you're likely out in the field or managing crews right now, so I'll keep this to 30 seconds. Do you have a quick moment?"

*If yes:*
> "We build job tracking and field mapping software specifically for custom harvesters and ag contractors. Most contracting owners we talk to tell us paper job sheets and delayed invoicing are their biggest headaches when they run multiple operators. We help operations eliminate paper notes, track operator hours and machine costs via GPS, and send out client invoices the same day the work is done. Are you currently using paper job sheets or an app to track your field work?"

*Listen, then move to the demo offer.*

## Objections and rebuttals
| Objection | Rebuttal |
|---|---|
| "Our operators aren't tech-savvy / won't use an app." | "That's exactly why Harvest Yield was designed for simplicity. If an operator can send a text message, they can log a job. It takes two taps to start recording a field, and turn-by-turn navigation guides them right to the boundary. Can I send a 2-minute video showing how simple the interface is?" |
| "We don't have good cell service in our rural fields." | "We built the mobile app specifically for rural ag conditions. Operators can record job hours, field mapping and input quantities completely offline. The data syncs automatically once they're back in cellular or Wi-Fi range." |
| "We already use Xero / QuickBooks / Excel." | "Harvest Yield integrates directly with Xero and accounting software. Instead of manually typing paper notes into your accounting system, it exports verified field records and operator hours straight into your invoices with one click." |
| "We're too busy right now with harvest/spraying season." | "That's precisely when paper job sheets get lost or delayed. You can set up a free trial in under 5 minutes without a credit card. Let's schedule a 10-minute check-in next week so you can test it on a single machine fleet." |
